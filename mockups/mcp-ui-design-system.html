Refined against: Apple Designing Fluid Interfaces (spring discipline, press-first, spatial consistency) · Emil Kowalski's design-engineering practice (easing curves, duration ceilings, blur crossfades, stagger) · UX usability heuristics (four states, undo-toasts, touch targets, progressive disclosure) · DexLab design system (tokens, scarce brand, quiet surfaces, ds-* discipline).
